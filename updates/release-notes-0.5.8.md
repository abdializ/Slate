# Slate 0.5.8

## New Features
- **lovesea-style Multi-Pane Split View (2–4 panes):** Split up to 4 tabs side by side in rounded card panes with 7pt gutters, subtle card shadows, and an outer focus border colored with your chosen Settings accent adapted to Light/Dark appearance.
- **Magnetic Even-Snap Dividers & Actions:** Drag any divider between panes with haptic snap-to-even (`15pt` snap / `20pt` unsnap), double-click a divider to make all panes even, or right-click a divider for Swap, Make Panes Even, Add Blank Pane, Separate Focused Pane, and Exit Split View.
- **Drag-to-Split Edge Bands & Live Multi-Pane Preview:** Dragging a tab over a page shows faint edge bands and a live multi-pane preview outlined in your Settings accent, including adding a 3rd or 4th pane to an existing split.
- **Blank-Pane Open-Tab Picker & Narrow Collapse:** Empty split panes offer an "Or bring in an open tab" list of open tabs; narrow windows (`< 250pt` per pane) cleanly collapse to the focused pane without losing the split.
- **Unified Split Tab Pill & Persistence:** 2–4 split tabs group into one rounded container with an accent outer line in the tab strip and persist across launches.

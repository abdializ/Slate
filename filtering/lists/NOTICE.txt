EasyList and EasyPrivacy network-rule extracts in this directory are derived
from https://easylist.to and remain under the EasyList project’s GNU GPLv3
terms for those data files. They are loaded at runtime as filter data, not
linked as program source. Cosmetic, scriptlet, redirect, and CSP rules were
stripped for Slate Shields V0.1.

`slate-network.txt` and `compat.txt` are Slate-authored network rules.

Slate’s matcher is original C++ and is not Brave’s adblock-rust engine.
adblock-rust (MPL-2.0) remains the intended replacement once a Rust toolchain
is part of the build. See docs/SHIELDS.md.
